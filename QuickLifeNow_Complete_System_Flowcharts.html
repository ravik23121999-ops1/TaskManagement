QuickLifeNow — Task Creation & Multi-Level Delegation Flowchart

How managers initiate tasks, select multiple assignees, and delegate sub-tasks down to team members

Core Workflow 1 Page 1 of 4
📝 How To Create & Delegate (Step-by-Step)
1

Click '+ New Task'

From any page, click the blue + New Task button in the top navigation bar or left sidebar.

2

Enter Task Details

Fill in the Task Title (e.g. Payment of Vinay Handloom), pick the Department (e.g. Finance), set the Due Date, and choose a Priority (Low, Medium, High, Urgent).

3

Select Assignees (1-to-Many Delegation)

Search and select the employees who will execute the task. You can select multiple people; the system automatically creates independent branches (e.g. 1.1 and 1.2) so each person has their own isolated checklist.

4

Further Delegation by Team Leads

If a team lead needs to break the task down for junior staff, they open their branch and click Delegate Work. This creates sub-tasks (e.g. 1.1.1 and 1.1.2) cleanly scaling up to 6 levels.

📌 Golden Rule of Delegation:

Team members only see their assigned branch. Lead A never sees or interferes with Lead B's work, ensuring focus, security, and clear accountability.

QuickLifeNow — Work Execution & Question Clarification Flowchart

How assignees execute deliverables, ask questions when info is missing, and submit proof of work

Core Workflow 2 Page 2 of 4
⚙️ How An Assignee Works (Step-by-Step)
1

Open 'My Action Required'

Every time you log in, check My Action Required in the sidebar. This shows tasks waiting directly for your action.

2

Inspect Requirements

Open the task and read the objectives, expected result, and any files attached by your manager.

3

Missing Info? Never Guess — Ask Instantly

If you need a login, invoice, or clarification, click Request Missing Details. Type your question. The task status moves to Waiting on Assigner without hurting your completion metrics.

4

Submit Work for Review

When your deliverables are ready, click Submit Work for Review. Attach your finished files (PDF, ZIP, Excel, images) and write a short summary note.

💡 Why Request Missing Details?

Guessing leads to rework. Using Request Missing Details officially pauses SLA pressure, sends an immediate notification to the assigner, and creates a clear audit trail.

QuickLifeNow — Review, Revision & Final Sign-Off Flowchart

How submissions are inspected, returned with notes if flawed, approved, and given final sign-off

Core Workflow 3 Page 3 of 4
🔍 Quality Review & Final Approval
1

Review Queue Alert

Reviewers receive an alert in the Review Queue showing the task code, assignee name, and submitted deliverables.

2

Download & Inspect Files

Download the files directly to inspect quality against the original acceptance criteria.

3

Returning for Revision

If changes are needed, click Return for Revision and write clear feedback. The assignee fixes the work and submits again.

4

Creator Final Sign-Off

Branch approval approves the member's piece. When all branches pass, the original task creator verifies the overall result and clicks Final Sign-Off.

🔒 The Two-Tier Quality Seal:

Team leads approve sub-branches, but only the Original Task Creator has the authority to issue the Final Sign-off that moves a task into Completed.

QuickLifeNow Task Management — Operational User Manual

Complete role reference guides, master search guide, and audit transparency

Reference Manual Page 4 of 4
1
Task Creator / Manager
Project Initiation & Sign-off
1. Click '+ New Task'

Enter task title, select department, priority, and completion deadline.

2. Select Multiple Assignees

Pick all team leads who will share responsibility. Each lead receives an isolated branch (1.1, 1.2).

3. Answer Clarification Questions

If an assignee requests missing details, provide the answers and files promptly.

4. Give Final Sign-off

Once all branches are approved, verify the combined deliverable and click Final Sign-Off.

Core Principle: You own the final seal. Team members cannot mark tasks complete until you verify deliverables.
2
Team Assignee
Work Execution & Sub-tasks
1. Monitor 'My Action Required'

All tasks requiring your active work appear here. Inspect requirements and deadlines.

2. Request Missing Details

Never work on guesswork. Click Request Missing Details to ask for missing assets.

3. Delegate to Sub-Leads

If needed, click Delegate Work to split work across junior members (1.1.1, 1.1.2).

4. Submit Finished Deliverables

Attach deliverable files (PDF, ZIP, DOCX) and click Submit Work for Review.

Assignee Principle: Always attach proof of work directly through the submission modal for fast approval.
3
Quality Reviewer
Quality Control & Verification
1. Monitor 'Review Queue'

All submissions awaiting verification appear here with submitter name and timestamp.

2. Download & Verify Files

Inspect files against original requirements. Check for completeness and accuracy.

3. Return for Revision If Flawed

Click Return for Revision and write clear notes explaining what needs fixing.

4. Approve Satisfactory Work

Click Approve Submission once deliverables meet all requirements.

Reviewer Principle: Constructive revision notes ensure team members make exact fixes on the first attempt.
