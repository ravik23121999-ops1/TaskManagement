2. Module-by-Module Step-by-Step Testing Journeys

Verify rapid role-switching, instant credentials filling, and security guard rejection.

1

Expected: The QuickLifeNow enterprise login portal renders with the 3D studio showcase and 3 one-click role cards.
2
Test 1-Click Administrator Login: Click the amber "Login" button on the Administrator card (Ravi Singh).
Expected: Instantly authenticates and redirects to the Executive Dashboard with your session token stored securely.
3
Test 1-Click Employee Login: Click the sign-out icon in the sidebar footer, then click the emerald "Login" button on the Team Contributor card (Karan Sharma).
Expected: Logs in as Karan Sharma and opens the personalized Employee Dashboard.

Negative Security Test: Log out, type ravi@company.com with password wrongpass, and click Sign In.
Expected: Rejected with HTTP 401 and an alert: "Invalid credentials".
