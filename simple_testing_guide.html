Let's Test Everything Step by Step

1

  2. On the login page, you will see 3 colorful cards for Admin, Manager, and Employee.

2
Change Your 3D Avatar or Upload Your Photo
  1. Look at your avatar on the top welcome banner, the top bar, and the bottom of the sidebar.
  2. Click the button that says "Change Avatar" (or click your picture at the bottom left).
  3. Click "Female" and hit Save → Your avatar turns into a 3D female avatar everywhere!
  4. Click "Male" and hit Save → It turns into a 3D male avatar.
  5. Want your real face? Click the "Upload Photo" tab, pick any picture from your laptop, and save!
What to look for: The photo updates live instantly on the banner, top bar, and sidebar without refreshing the page.
3
Test "Work Alone" Mode (Clean 3-Stage Pipeline & Instant Final Approval)
  1. In the left sidebar, click the blue button: "+ Create New Task".
  2. Type a task name: "Review Q4 Budget & Audit Notes".
  3. Under Handling Method, pick "Work Alone" (I will execute this myself).
  4. Click "Create Task".
  5. Open the newly created task (code T-001).
Notice the smart Work-Alone workflow:
  • Streamlined 3-Stage Stepper: Displays only 1. Initiated → 2. In Progress → 3. Completed. The redundant "Evaluation / Under Review" and "Sign-off" steps are hidden because no 3rd-party review is required!
  • Single Direct Action: Only the green "Final Approval & Complete" button is shown. There is no confusing "Submit for Review" button because you are the worker, reviewer, and final approver.
4
Complete the Work-Alone Task & Watch It Exit "Pending" and "All Tasks"
  1. On the task page for T-001, click the green "Final Approval & Complete" button.
  2. Confirm the prompt. The status instantly changes to "Completed" (100% progress).
  3. Now, click on "Pending Tasks" in the sidebar → T-001 does NOT appear here!
  4. Click on "All Tasks" in the sidebar → T-001 does NOT appear here either! (All Tasks only shows open active tasks).
  5. Now click on "Completed" in the sidebar → T-001 is waiting here safe and sound!
What to look for: Completed tasks never linger in Pending or All Tasks; they move immediately to the Completed phase where they belong.

Multi-Tier Hierarchy Review (Upper Branch / Assigner & Creator Evaluation)
  1. Log in as Employee: Log in as Rohit Sharma (or Karan). Open task T-002.
  2. Notice Self-Review Is Blocked: Click "Submit for Review" and submit deliverables. Notice Rohit does NOT see a "Review" button, and this task is NOT in his Review Queue — because no employee can review their own work!
  3. Ask Assigner via Comments: If something is missing, notice the blue banner on the Comments tab: "Need clarification? Post a comment to discuss directly with your assigner." Posting a comment notifies his assigner immediately.
