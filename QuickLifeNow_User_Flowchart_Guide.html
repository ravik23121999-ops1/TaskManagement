The Complete Journey: How Work Moves in QuickLifeNow

Every assignment follows a disciplined, transparent 5-step lifecycle ensuring accountability, zero lost tasks, and full reviewer verification.

📋 Operational Guide (How To Use)
1

Initiation & Goal Setting

Click + New Task. Set the title, department, priority, and assign to 1 or more leads. The system assigns a unique Task Code (e.g. T-010).

2

Active Execution & Delegation

Assignees see the task in their My Action Required tab. If large, they can sub-delegate to team members (1.1, 1.2) or work directly.

3

Question Loop (No Blockers)

If anything is unclear, the assignee clicks Request Missing Details. The assigner provides instant clarity so work resumes without delay.

4

Review & Quality Control

Assignee uploads files & submits. The reviewer inspects deliverables. If fixes are needed, they return it with constructive notes; if good, they approve.

5

Final Creator Sign-off

When all sub-branches pass review, the task creator performs final verification and clicks Final Sign-off. Status turns to Completed.

💡 Golden Principle

A task is only marked Completed when the original creator signs off. Team members never sign off on their own work, ensuring strict audit compliance.

Task Creation & Delegation: How Large Projects Are Broken Down

How a single company goal is systematically delegated into sub-tasks with independent branch owners and isolated responsibility.

🌲 How Delegation Works in Practice
1

Initiating Multiple Leads

When creating a task, you can pick multiple assignees (e.g. Aman & Pooja). QuickLifeNow creates isolated sub-branches 1.1 and 1.2.

2

Further Delegation (Leads to Team)

Team leads open their branch and click Delegate Work. They assign sub-tasks (e.g. 1.1.1 to Rohit, 1.1.2 to Neha).

3

Strict Permission Boundaries

Sub-assignees only see the exact deliverables assigned to them. They cannot alter sibling branches or edit parent goals.

4

Real-time Hierarchy Tree

On every task page, clicking the Hierarchy Flow tab shows an interactive visual tree diagram displaying all levels and live branch statuses.

🛡️ Zero Interference Guarantee

When Lead A finishes work, Lead B is completely unaffected. Each branch operates on its own timeline until all work merges at the parent review stage.

Assignee Execution: What To Do When Instructions Are Unclear

How the built-in "Request Missing Details" protocol eliminates wrong assumptions and guarantees quality output before submission.

❓ Handling Missing Information
1

Inspect Goals in "My Action"

When you log in, open My Action Required. Review the requirements, attachments, and deadline carefully.

2

Never Guess — Ask Instantly

If you lack a file, password, or requirement, click Request Missing Details. Type your query. The task safely pauses without penalizing your due date.

3

Assigner Clarification

The assigner receives an alert in their queue. They provide the answer and attachments. The task instantly reactivates in your active list.

4

Submitting Clean Deliverables

When finished, click Submit Work for Review. Attach your final documents (PDF, Excel, images, code files) and write a short completion note.

🚀 Best Practice

Always attach deliverable files directly through the submit modal. This ties proof of work directly to your submission timestamp for the reviewer.

Quality Control & Sign-Off: How Work Is Inspected and Approved

How submissions are rigorously checked, returned with revision notes if flawed, or signed off by the original task creator.

🔍 How Review & Sign-Off Works
1

Review Queue Notification

When deliverables are submitted, the designated reviewer (or assigner) sees the submission in their Review Queue.

2

Direct File Verification

The reviewer clicks to download files directly. They evaluate the deliverable against the original instructions and quality criteria.

3

Revision Feedback Loop

If anything requires changes, click Return for Revision and write specific comments. The assignee fixes the work and re-submits.

4

The 2-Tier Sign-off Seal

Branch approval verifies the member's piece. When all branches are approved, the main creator verifies the whole project and signs off.

📜 Full Accountability Log

Every review decision, return comment, and sign-off is stamped with user identity, date, and exact time in the immutable Work Logs audit trail.

Collaboration, Handover & Auditing: Complete System Transparency

How team members communicate in real-time, how work is transferred if an employee takes leave, and how managers audit all activity.

💬 Communication & Transparency
1

Task-Specific Messaging

No lost emails or third-party chat confusion. Click Messages to chat directly within the context of any active task branch.

2

Employee Leave & Transfers

If an assignee goes on leave or has emergency workload, the lead clicks Transfer Branch. The new owner picks up right where they left off.

3

Putting Tasks On Hold

If a client or external dependency is delayed, managers can click Put on Hold. The countdown pauses until formally resumed.

4

Regulatory Work Logs

Open Work Logs to view an audit trail of every status change, review decision, and submission with exact user attribution.

📊 Management Oversight

Managers and executives can filter Work Logs by user, department, or date range to generate weekly delivery reports and evaluate team throughput.
